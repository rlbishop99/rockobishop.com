Minute Mayhem

← Back to Projects
Play Minute Mayhem on itch.io ↗

Minute Mayhem is a spiritual successor to Mushroom Mix-up, the classic Party game from the original Mario Party. You are stock on a set of interlocking Gear platforms and over time the platforms will start to drop into the water, leaving you with less room to move around. Enemies will spawn and try to push you off, so it is an endless survival game! You can crank a lever to raise up all of the dropped platforms, but over time the frequency with which enemies spawn and platforms lower will make it harder and harder to survive.

This game was fun to make and ended up being pretty accurate to the GDD my Team of 3 Devs wrote before we began programming! I learned a lot about properly designing and scoping a project this go around, and I think it shows in the final product. The game is simple and goofy, but still a lot of fun!
